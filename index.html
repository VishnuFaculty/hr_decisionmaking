The Divergent Branch Head

First-Time Leader's Decision & Creativity Simulator

As a new branch head, traditional convergent thinking (finding one "right" answer) isn't enough. This simulator tests your ability to generate multiple solutions, empathize with stakeholders, and visualize complex banking challenges before making a decision.

Welcome to the Simulator

You are the newly appointed head of a critical public sector bank branch.

You will face 10 realistic management scenarios encompassing business growth, staff motivation, NPA management, and customer service. Select the approach you would instinctively take. Once selected, your decision is locked, and you will see how it aligns with proven divergent thinking methodologies.

The 5 Pillars of Divergent Thinking

Based on cognitive science and effective management practices, these tools help break mental stalemates:

Mind Mapping
Start in the centre and add nodes to connect branches/units together, helping your brain work by association[cite: 1].
Brainstorming
Focus on quantity versus quality to generate as many ideas as possible[cite: 1].
Role Playing
Put yourself in the shoes of a different character to see the branch from a new perspective[cite: 1].
Journaling & Free Writing
Write non-stop in a stream-of-consciousness way to clarify thinking and generate ideas[cite: 1].
Art & Visualization
Create graphs and illustrations to help you think outside the box and tap into creativity[cite: 1].
