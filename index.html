The Cognitive Branch Head

First-Time Leader's Simulator for Decision Making & Bias Mitigation

As a new branch head, you must navigate high-pressure targets, staff dynamics, and complex customer demands. This simulator tests your ability to generate divergent solutions while overcoming dangerous cognitive biases (Halo, Horn, Anchoring, Hindsight, Expediency, and Conformity).

Welcome to the Simulator

You face 25 realistic scenarios encompassing NPA management, credit appraisal, staff discipline, and customer service. Select the approach you would instinctively take. Once selected, your decision is locked.

The 6 Pillars of Cognitive Leadership

Break mental stalemates and mitigate cognitive biases using these tools:

Mind Mapping
Connecting isolated nodes (people, cash flows, processes) to see the bigger picture.
Brainstorming
Generating massive quantities of ideas without premature judgment to find hidden solutions.
Role Playing
Stepping into the shoes of customers, staff, or auditors to build empathy and de-escalate.
Journaling / Drafting
Free-writing to clarify complex thoughts and bypass emotional triggers before acting.
Visualization
Creating graphs, charts, and spatial maps to objectively analyze a situation.
Bias Awareness
Actively recognizing and neutralizing Halo, Horn, Anchoring, Conformity, Expediency, and Hindsight biases.
